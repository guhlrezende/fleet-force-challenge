# Fleet Force — AI Design Instructions

## MUST
- Treat supplied Fleet Force logo files as authoritative brand assets.
- Use #0750ED as primary brand/action blue, #2E2D2D as graphite, and white/neutral surfaces as the default canvas.
- Use Inter for UI/document text; use bold/extra-bold display weights for headings. Never recreate the logo wordmark with Inter.
- Use an 8 px spacing rhythm, 12 px default control radius and 16 px default card radius.
- Maintain clear hierarchy: context -> headline -> main evidence -> support -> action.
- Use directional geometry only to communicate movement, progress, route or flow.
- Keep content-heavy layouts mostly neutral; blue must remain a scarce emphasis resource.
- Maintain WCAG AA contrast and visible focus states.

## SHOULD
- Use 12-column / 1200 px desktop grids, 8-column tablet, 4-column mobile.
- Prefer borders to heavy shadows; use shadows only for real elevation.
- Use authentic fleet, mobility, logistics, road and operational imagery with forward visual direction.
- Make metrics, comparisons and charts more prominent than decorative imagery.
- Use chart series in this order: #0750ED, #16B8F3, #7657FF, #36B37E, #FF9F43, #2E2D2D.

## MAY
- Use a restrained blue-to-deep-blue gradient for hero/cover backgrounds only.
- Use 8-16° diagonal cuts and arrow wedges as secondary motifs.
- Use the compact symbol for app icons, avatars and small signatures.

## MUST NOT
- Do not distort, rotate, redraw, retype, shadow or arbitrarily recolor the logo.
- Do not introduce colors outside the defined palette without explicit approval.
- Do not copy Sem Parar logos, wording, illustrations, proprietary assets or branded compositions.
- Do not use rainbow gradients, 3D charts, glassmorphism, excessive glow, bubbly shapes or decorative arrows without function.
- Do not communicate status with color alone.
- Do not place dense text over busy photography.

## Artifact Defaults
Interface: white canvas, 80-128 px desktop section gaps, 24 px card padding, 44-48 px controls. Presentation: 16:9, <=2 emphasis colors, one message/slide. Document: A4, 18-20 mm margins, blue section markers. Social: short headline + one focal visual/metric + one directional motif.
